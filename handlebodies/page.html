Surface and π

Follows the solutions along the path instead of re-solving at every step. Much quicker at high degree, where the exact route is not practical; leave it off for small surfaces, where the exact route is instant and exact. The strongest check there is, but that one solve can cost many times the tracking it checks -- about twenty times, on a degree-14 surface -- so it is off unless you ask for it.

Write powers as **, the imaginary unit as I. Decimals are read exactly: 0.3 means 3/10.
